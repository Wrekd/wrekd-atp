1 VISUAL INSPECTION (No Power)
What you're checking: Before applying any power, visually inspect the entire aircraft for physical damage, loose components, or assembly errors. This prevents powering on a damaged unit.
  • Frame Integrity
    Inspect all four arms for cracks, chips, or damage. Carbon fiber should be smooth with no delamination. Check where arms meet the center body - these joints take the most stress.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Motor Attachment
    Check all 4 motor screws are present and tight. Motors should not wobble when gently pushed. Spin each motor by hand - should rotate smoothly with no grinding, clicking, or resistance.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Electronics Stack
    The flight controller and ESC (speed controller) stack in the center should be firmly mounted. No loose screws, no visible damage to circuit boards. Wires should not be pinched or frayed.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Camera & VTX
    Camera should be mounted securely in the front of the frame. Lens should be clean (no fingerprints, scratches, or dust). Video transmitter (VTX) antenna connector should be hand-tight.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Antenna Installation
    VTX antenna at rear should be firmly attached. Antenna should point upward or at 45° angle - never directly at the ground. Check antenna cable is not kinked or damaged.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Wire Routing
    All wires should be secured with clips or zip ties. No loose wires that could contact spinning propellers. Motor wires routed along arms, not hanging free.
    Pass
    Fail
  • XT60 Power Connector
    The yellow XT60 connector (main power) should have clean, undamaged pins. No burn marks, melting, or corrosion. Connector should be securely attached to its wires.

2 BATTERY INSTALLATION
⚠️
SAFETY FIRST Do NOT connect the battery yet. This section is about proper physical mounting only. Power-on tests come next.
  • Battery Pad Present
    A non-slip battery pad should be on top of the frame where the battery sits. This prevents the battery from sliding during flight and protects the frame from vibration.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Battery Strap Installed
    Battery strap should be threaded through the frame slots. The velcro (hook) side should face DOWN toward the battery, soft (loop) side faces OUT.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Battery Position
    Place battery centered on the frame, aligned with the long axis of the drone. Battery should sit flat with no wobble. Adjust position until drone balances level.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Strap Tension
    Strap must be pulled TIGHT. The battery should not move at all when you shake the drone. A loose battery will shift during flight, causing balance issues or crashes.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Cable Position
    Battery power cable (red/black wires with yellow XT60) should exit toward the REAR of the drone. Balance lead (white connector with multiple wires) should be tucked safely, not dangling.
    Pass
    Fail

REMOVE ALL PROPELLERS Do NOT install propellers until Section 6. Motors will spin during testing. Exposed motors without props are safe. Motors WITH props can cause serious injury.
  • Props Removed Confirmed
    Verify ALL FOUR propellers are removed before proceeding. Check each motor - there should be just the bare motor bell visible, no propeller attached.

    Fail
  • No Smoke or Smell
    Immediately after connecting, check for any smoke, sparks, burning smell, or excessive heat. If ANY of these occur, DISCONNECT BATTERY IMMEDIATELY.
    Pass
    Fail

    Fail

4 RADIO LINK TEST (Props OFF)
What you're testing: The radio link connects the pilot's controller to the drone. Without a working radio link, the drone cannot be controlled. These tests verify the controller and drone can communicate.
  • Receiver Bound
    The receiver LED (on the drone) should show solid or slow blinking when the transmitter is on. If LED is fast-blinking or off, the receiver is not bound to the transmitter.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Stick Inputs Respond
    Looking at the OSD in goggles, move each control stick. The on-screen indicators should move corresponding to stick movements. All 4 channels (throttle, yaw, pitch, roll) must respond.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Arm Switch Functions
    The arm switch (usually a 2-position switch) should be configured. Flipping it should change the armed status shown in OSD. DO NOT actually arm yet - just verify the switch is recognized.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Failsafe Test
    Turn OFF the transmitter while drone is powered. The drone should NOT arm or fly away. It should either do nothing or show a "failsafe" warning. Turn transmitter back on - link should restore.
    Pass
    Fail
  • RSSI Displayed
    RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator) should show in the OSD. At close range it should read 90-100%. This tells the pilot how strong the control signal is during flight.
    Pass
    Fail

5 MOTOR TEST (Props OFF)
⚠️
SECURE THE DRONE Hold the drone firmly or secure it to a test stand. Motors will spin at high speed. Keep fingers, hair, and loose clothing away from motors.
  • Arm the Drone
    With throttle at ZERO, flip the arm switch. Motors should begin spinning at idle speed. If motors don't spin, check that throttle is fully down and arm switch is configured.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Motor 1 (Front-Left) - CCW
    Increase throttle slightly. Motor 1 should spin counter-clockwise when viewed from above. Check for smooth operation, no vibration, grinding, or unusual noise.

    Fail
  • Motor 2 (Front-Right) - CW
    Motor 2 should spin clockwise when viewed from above. Check for smooth operation, no vibration, grinding, or unusual noise.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Motor 3 (Rear-Left) - CW
    Motor 3 should spin clockwise when viewed from above. Check for smooth operation, no vibration, grinding, or unusual noise.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Motor 4 (Rear-Right) - CCW
    Motor 4 should spin counter-clockwise when viewed from above. Check for smooth operation, no vibration, grinding, or unusual noise.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Throttle Response
    Move throttle up and down. All motors should respond together, speeding up and slowing down in sync. Response should be immediate with no delay.
    Pass
    Fail

6 PROPELLER INSTALLATION
⚠️
BATTERY MUST BE DISCONNECTED Before installing propellers, ensure the battery is unplugged. Never install or remove props while the drone is powered.
Propeller Direction: Props are marked CW (clockwise) or CCW (counter-clockwise). CW props go on CW motors (2 & 3). CCW props go on CCW motors (1 & 4). Wrong prop direction = drone won't fly.
  • Battery Disconnected
    Confirm battery is unplugged before handling propellers. This prevents accidental motor spin which can cause serious injury.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Props Undamaged
    Inspect each propeller for cracks, chips, or warping. Even small damage affects flight performance. Replace any damaged props.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Correct Prop Matching
    CCW props on Motors 1 & 4 (front-left, rear-right). CW props on Motors 2 & 3 (front-right, rear-left). Check markings on props match motor rotation.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Props Fully Seated
    Push each prop down firmly onto the motor shaft. There should be no gap between the prop and motor. Prop should not wobble or spin on the shaft.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Prop Nuts Tight
    If props use nuts (not press-fit), tighten each nut firmly. Props should not move or rotate on the shaft. Do not overtighten - snug is sufficient.
    Pass
    Fail
  • Props Clear Frame
    Spin each prop by hand. Props should rotate freely without hitting the frame, wires, or other props. Clear at least 5mm gap from any obstacle.
    Pass
    Fail

Acceptance Test Procedure

FPV Drone Pre-Delivery Verification
POWERUS

1 Unboxing Inspection

Packaging intact, no crushing or water damage
All items present per packing list
No visible damage to airframe
Props included and undamaged

2 Visual Inspection (No Power)

Frame arms secure, no cracks
Motors spin freely by hand, no grinding
Motor screws tight
ESC/FC stack secure
Antenna connections secure (VTX, RX)
Camera mounted securely, lens clean
Battery strap/pad in place
XT60/XT30 connector undamaged

3 Power-On Test (Props OFF)

Remove all propellers before this test
Connect battery, listen for startup tones
No smoke, burning smell, or sparks
LEDs indicate normal status
VTX powers on (check with goggles)
Camera image visible in goggles
OSD displays correctly (battery voltage, etc.)

4 Radio Link Test (Props OFF)

Receiver binds to transmitter
Stick inputs respond in Betaflight/OSD
All channels mapped correctly
Failsafe triggers when TX powered off
RSSI displays in OSD

5 Motor Test (Props OFF)

Secure drone to bench or hold firmly - motors will spin
Motor 1 (front-left) spins CCW
Motor 2 (front-right) spins CW
Motor 3 (rear-left) spins CW
Motor 4 (rear-right) spins CCW
No abnormal vibration or noise
Motors stop cleanly (no cogging)

6 Arming Test (Props OFF)

Arm switch/stick arms the drone
Motors spin at idle when armed
Disarm stops motors immediately
Arm blocked if throttle not at zero

7 Propeller Installation

Props matched to correct motors (CW/CCW)
Props fully seated and secured
No prop damage (chips, cracks)
Props clear frame when spinning

8 Hover Test (Outdoors)

Open area, low wind, no people nearby
Drone lifts off smoothly
Stable hover at 2m altitude
No oscillations or toilet-bowling
Responds correctly to pitch/roll/yaw
Throttle response linear
Lands without issues

9 FPV System Flight Test

Video feed stable during hover
No video breakup at 50m range
No video breakup at 100m range
OSD data updates correctly in flight
No interference or noise in video

10 Full Flight Test

Forward flight stable
Turns/banks smooth
3+ minute flight without issues
Battery voltage stable under load
No overheating (motors, ESC, VTX)
